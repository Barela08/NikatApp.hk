NIKAT ANIMATION SYSTEM (FINAL – PERFORMANCE SAFE)

---

ANIMATION PRINCIPLE:

* Animation ka purpose: guide user, not impress
* Fast > Fancy
* Subtle > Loud

Rule:
Agar animation UX improve nahi karta → REMOVE

---

GLOBAL SETTINGS:

Duration:

* Fast: 150ms
* Normal: 200–250ms
* Slow: max 300ms

Easing:

* ease-out (default)
* no bounce, no overshoot

---

BUTTON ANIMATION:

On Tap:

* Scale: 1 → 0.96
* Duration: 100ms

On Release:

* Back to normal (smooth)

Optional:

* Soft glow pulse (primary button only)

---

CARD ANIMATION:

On Load:

* Fade + slight slide up

Values:

* Opacity: 0 → 1
* TranslateY: 20px → 0
* Duration: 200ms

Delay:

* Stagger (50ms per card)

---

NAVIGATION TRANSITION:

Screen Change:

* Slide from right
* Duration: 200ms

Back Action:

* Slide from left

NO:

* Zoom transitions
* Flip animations

---

SEARCH INTERACTION:

On Focus:

* Border glow (#00FF88)
* Slight expand (scale 1.00 → 1.02)

Mic Tap:

* Pulse animation
* Waveform while listening

After Voice Input:

* Smooth text fill (typing effect optional)

---

LOADING STATE:

Use Skeleton Loader ONLY

Skeleton Animation:

* Shimmer left → right
* Duration: 1.2s loop

NO:

* Spinner as primary loader

---

ICON MICRO-ANIMATIONS:

Allowed:

* Tap → slight scale (0.95)
* Active → color change

NOT allowed:

* Continuous spinning
* Random motion

---

FEEDBACK ANIMATIONS:

Success:

* Quick fade-in + small scale pop

Error:

* Subtle shake (horizontal)
* Duration: 150ms

---

LIST SCROLL:

* Smooth scroll
* No heavy parallax

Optional:

* Lazy load fade-in items

---

BOTTOM NAVBAR:

Active Tab:

* Color change (#00FF88)
* Slight scale (1 → 1.1)

---

EMPTY STATE:

* Icon fade-in
* Text fade-in (delay 100ms)

---

PERFORMANCE RULES (CRITICAL):

* Use hardware acceleration
* Avoid heavy Lottie files
* Keep animations under 60fps
* Disable animation on low-end devices

---

WHAT TO NEVER DO:

❌ Long animations (>300ms)
❌ Multiple animations at once
❌ Random motion (distracting)
❌ Heavy graphics-based animation
❌ Laggy transitions

---

ADVANCED FEEL COMES FROM:

* Instant response
* Smooth transitions
* Clean UI
* Zero lag

NOT from:

* Fancy animation
* Over effects

---

FINAL RULE:

User ko animation notice nahi hona chahiye
Sirf experience smooth lagna chahiye
